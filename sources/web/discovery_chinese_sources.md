# Chinese-language sources on the Silk Nebula (丝绸星云)

All accessed 2026-11-16 through Tavily/Exa extracts. Quotes are verbatim; English translations are mine.

## 1. Guo-You Sun (孙国佑, SWONS member and Wu+2026 co-author), blog post dated 2024-05-21

URL: https://sunguoyou.lamost.org/sichou.html (title: "热烈祝贺中国天文爱好者发现新的星云--丝绸星云！"); listed on https://sunguoyou.lamost.org/new-discovery

> "2023年12月份，SWONS项目在针对冬季星空的窄带巡天中发现了一条绚丽的［OIII］条带，消息一经公布就引起广泛关注，SWONS团队随即对本次发现展开更深入的研究。（SWONS团队成员有：贾淞寓，张超，武朝剑，张卫国，邱虹云，孙国佑）"

Translation: "In December 2023, the SWONS project found a gorgeous [OIII] band in its narrowband survey of the winter sky. The news attracted wide attention once announced, and the SWONS team immediately began deeper study. (SWONS team members: Jia Songyu, Zhang Chao, Wu Chaojian, Zhang Weiguo, Qiu Hongyun, Sun Guoyou)"

> "近日，这条绚丽的［OIII］条带被小组名为"丝绸星云"，有关项目的简要介绍和业余设备测定的目标简要特征发布在2024年第四期《中国国家天文》杂志上。"

Translation: "Recently the team named this [OIII] band the 'Silk Nebula'. A brief introduction to the project and the object's main characteristics, as measured with amateur equipment, were published in the 2024 No. 4 issue of *China National Astronomy* (中国国家天文)."

Notes: the month given here (December 2023) differs from the paper (November 2023). The member list includes two NAOC/LAMOST MRS-N professionals, Zhang Chao (张超) and Wu Chaojian (武朝剑), who are the paper's corresponding authors. I have not seen the magazine issue (data gap).

## 2. Xinmin Evening News (新民晚报, reporter 郜阳), via Shangguan News (上观新闻), 2026-11-16 03:53

URL: https://www.shobserver.com/news/detail?id=1214625 — "公众天文研究立功！天文爱好者发现的"丝绸星云"，原来是超新星爆炸现场". The same text is mirrored at https://feng.ifeng.com/c/8y63sUUKpcN (ifeng, 2026-11-16 11:53 +08) and on finance.sina.cn (2026-11-16).

Key passages:
> "该科研成果已发表在国际天文期刊《天文学期刊》（The Astronomical Journal）上。国家天文台研究生吴逸霏和星空摄影师贾淞寓为共同第一作者，国家天文台武朝剑研究员和张超为共同通讯作者。"
(The result is published in AJ. NAOC graduate student Wu Yifei and sky photographer Jia Songyu are co-first authors. NAOC researchers Wu Chaojian and Zhang Chao are co-corresponding authors.)

> "2023年初，中国天文爱好者团队在双子座天区捕捉到一条若隐若现的南北向[O III]细丝。"
("In early 2023, a Chinese amateur team captured a faint north–south [O III] filament in Gemini.") This contradicts the paper: SWONS *started* in early 2023 and the detection was in November 2023.

> "...得出它距离地球仅约160秒差距（约520光年）。其10度的角尺度跨度对应约28秒差距（约91光年）的物理尺度 ... 这次超新星爆炸大约发生在6.8万年前"
(~160 pc ≈ 520 ly; 10° ↔ ~28 pc ≈ 91 ly; the supernova happened about 68,000 years ago.)

The headline presents the SNR interpretation as established ("原来是超新星爆炸现场", "it turns out to be a supernova explosion site"). The paper itself calls the SNR origin "possible" and does not exclude DIG or an ionized bubble. The article does not mention the independent discoverers credited in the AJ acknowledgments.
